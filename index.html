Phone or tablet + USB + DAW

Use Chrome on Android with USB MIDI support.

  1. Connect a USB data cable to your computer. On Android, choose MIDI in the USB options.
  2. Connect MIDI here and allow access.
  3. In your DAW, select the phone's MIDI input, load an MPE instrument and arm the track. Send a test note, then play.
No USB MIDI output?

Check that the cable carries data, not just power, and that your phone offers USB MIDI mode. A known or single output connects automatically; otherwise choose the USB output. If the computer recognizes your phone as MIDI, no virtual MIDI bridge is needed. Android guide

iPhone or iPad?

Safari on iPhone and iPad does not support Web MIDI. This USB-to-DAW route is unavailable there, even from the home screen. You can still play the browser sound. Browser compatibility

Playing on the computer itself?

Mac: enable the built-in IAC Driver in Audio MIDI Setup, then select the same bus here and in your DAW. Apple guide

Windows: use a MIDI route already exposed by the system or compatible hardware. A web page cannot create a virtual MIDI port. This is separate from connecting an Android phone by USB.

Notes bend incorrectly or no sound?

Match the receiving instrument to MPE lower zone: master channel 1, note channels 2-6, pitch bend +/-48 semitones, slide CC74. Lock fixes pitch to the scale; it still sends per-note pitch bend.

Check the selected input, track monitoring, instrument and volume. An open MIDI output does not confirm sound in the DAW. If this browser has no Web MIDI, you can still use local sound.
